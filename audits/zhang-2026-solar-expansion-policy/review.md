All nine checked estimates (Table 1, Table S7, Table S12 column 8 and Table 2) reproduce to the reported precision in Python from the authors' public Stata package, including sample sizes.

The headline estimate (Table 1, column 3) is robust to winsorizing, clustering by city, province-by-month fixed effects and dropping early years. It changes sign and loses significance when the number of birdwatchers or total birdwatching hours is controlled for, which suggests that the outcome may reflect observation effort. Two published comments raise the same point.
